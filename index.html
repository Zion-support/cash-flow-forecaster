Cash Flow Forecaster

A proposed 13-week cash-flow planning process. Compare scenarios only after checking opening balances, expected receipts and approved payments.

Current implementation status

This is documentation and an informational page, not an implemented forecasting engine. Bank/ERP integration, production readiness, SSO, RBAC, encryption controls and forecast accuracy have not been established here.

Not financial or investment advice. Do not upload account credentials or customer payment data. No payment, purchase or bank connection is authorized.

Define a responsible pilot

  1. Name the treasury owner and reconcile a manually checked opening balance.
  2. Use synthetic weekly receipts and approved-payment assumptions; compare base and downside scenarios.
  3. Measure forecast error and missing data. Stop if balances cannot be reconciled; never initiate payments automatically.
